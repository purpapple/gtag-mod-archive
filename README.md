# gtag-mod-archive

this is a collection of mods made by yours truly, dont flame me im js learning c# (mods updated for gorilla tag mid 2026, unity 6000.2.9f1)
will prolly change bc i gotta get good at c# so i can be a cool kid like the dude who skids off me (greenfs)

- Drivemod: Hold right trigger and youll drive wherever youre looking
- Fly: Hold right primary (usually A) and youll fly in the direction youre looking
- Platforms: Grip on either controller makes an invisible platform
- Speedmod: Right primary is a toggle, click once to turn it on and again to turn it off
- UpAndDown: Right trigger makes you go up, left makes you go down
